🚀 NextGenTeens Platform Tutorial

Your complete guide to navigating and using the NextGenTeens Youth Development Platform

🎯 Getting Started

Welcome to NextGenTeens! This platform is designed to help teenagers grow spiritually, develop leadership skills, and build meaningful relationships through structured programs and mentorship.

Account Creation

  • Visit the registration page and select your role (Student, Mentor, or Administrator)
  • Fill in your personal information including name, email, and other required details
  • Create a secure password and complete the registration process
  • Check your email for verification (if required)
  • Log in using your credentials on the login page

First Login

After logging in for the first time, you'll be directed to your dashboard based on your role:

  • Students: Student Dashboard with assignments, progress tracking, and activities
  • Mentors: Mentor Dashboard with student management and review tools
  • Admins: Admin Dashboard with platform-wide management capabilities
💡 Tip: Bookmark your dashboard for quick access to the platform!

👨‍🎓 Student Guide

As a student, you have access to a comprehensive set of tools to support your growth journey.

Dashboard Navigation

📋 Tasks

View and submit assignments for your enrolled programs

🏆 Achievements

Track your earned badges and accomplishments

📊 Leaderboard

See your ranking among other students

👤 Profile

Manage your personal information and settings

Submitting Assignments

  • Navigate to the Tasks section from your dashboard
  • Select an assignment you want to complete
  • Review the assignment requirements and deadline
  • Complete the task (text, image, or video submission)
  • Submit your work for mentor review
  • Wait for feedback and approval status

Viewing Mentor Feedback

After your submission is reviewed, you can view mentor comments:

  • Go to your Tasks section
  • Look for assignments with "Approved" or "Rejected" status
  • Mentor feedback will be displayed below the assignment details
  • Use the feedback to improve future submissions

Daily Practice

Track your spiritual growth through daily practices:

  • Mark your daily Bible reading
  • Log your prayer time
  • Add journal notes for reflection
  • Build consistency streaks for achievements

Games & Learning Lab

Engage with interactive learning experiences:

  • Access the Learning Lab from your dashboard
  • Play educational games to reinforce learning
  • Earn points and track your progress
  • Compete with peers on leaderboards

✨ Key Features

🎯 Student Growth Index (SGI)

The SGI is a comprehensive performance metric that tracks your overall development:

  • Attendance (15%): Regular participation in sessions
  • Assignments (15%): Timely completion of tasks
  • Practice Videos (15%): Practical skill demonstration
  • Games & Quizzes (10%): Interactive learning engagement
  • Leadership Activities (10%): Leadership skill development
  • Spiritual Development (10%): Faith growth activities
  • Mentor Evaluation (15%): Assessment from mentors
  • AI Growth Assessment (10%): AI-powered progress analysis

🏆 Achievement System

Earn badges for reaching milestones:

  • Perfect Attendance - No missed sessions
  • Consistency Champion - On-time submissions for 4 weeks
  • Leadership Star - Outstanding leadership in activities
  • Voice Master - Excellence in voice training
  • Scripture Champion - Memorizing 10+ verses
  • Prayer Warrior - 30 consecutive prayer days
  • Kingdom Builder - Community impact achievements
  • CTFS Graduate - Completing the 12-week program

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

Can I change my role after registration?

Role changes require admin approval. Contact your program administrator for role modification requests.

How are strikes calculated?

Each unexcused absence generates one strike. Strike levels: 0-4 (Active), 5-7 (Warning), 8-9 (Critical), 10+ (Suspension review).

What happens when I submit an assignment?

Your submission goes to mentors for review. You'll receive feedback and an approval status. Approved submissions count toward your SGI score.

How do I access the AI Coach?

Click on "AI Coach" from your dashboard sidebar to start a conversation with your AI coaching assistant.

Can I view other students' progress?

You can view the leaderboard to see rankings, but individual student details are private to protect confidentiality.

How do I earn achievements?

Achievements are automatically earned when you meet specific criteria like attendance streaks, assignment consistency, or program completion.

What if I have technical issues?

Contact your program mentor or administrator for technical support. They can help resolve platform access or functionality issues.
