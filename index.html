About Us

JTF Youth Development

Committed to raising disciplined, purpose-driven, spiritually grounded, and socially impactful young people.

👁️

Vision

To raise a generation of disciplined, purpose-driven, spiritually grounded, and impactful young people who positively influence society through leadership, character, service, and excellence.

🎯

Mission

To provide teenagers with structured opportunities for growth, mentorship, leadership development, spiritual formation, practical learning, and community engagement through a technology-driven platform.

💡

Core Values

Faith, Leadership, Excellence, Integrity, Service, Community, Growth, and Purpose-driven living.
